Human-centered AX(HAX) Lab 

Human-centered AX(HAX) Lab is a research laboratory dedicated to advancing Extended Reality (XR) technologies, including Virtual Reality (VR), Augmented Reality (AR), and Mixed Reality (MR), as well as Metaverse. Our primary focus is on enhancing Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) by exploring seamless and intuitive interactions between humans and digital contents.
