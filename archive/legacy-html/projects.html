Projects

 

Ongoing Projects

XR-Physical AI-based Human–Digital Twin Interaction for Expanding the Everyday Perception of the Visually Impaired 

Ministry of Science and ICT, NRF (2026 ~ 2029)

  • Development of XR-Physical AI-based human–digital twin interaction technologies to enhance environmental perception and safe mobility for visually impaired individuals, including 3D spatial reconstruction, XR-based mobility training, and real-time environmental awareness support. 

Large-Scale AI-based defense product design and maintenance service demonstration project

Ministry of Science and ICT, NIA (2024 ~ 2026)

  • The project aims to develop and demonstrate a hyper-scale AI convergence service to enhance the productivity of defense product (military supply) manufacturing companies by leveraging hyper-scale AI and spatial computing (XR) technologies. 

Automated Reconstruction of Fire Scenes and VR-based Immersive Investigation Technology 

National Forensic Service (2025 ~ 2027)

  • This project aims to develop immersive and interactive technologies tailored for forensic investigations in fire scenes. The core objectives include the development of context-aware interaction technologies for immersive field investigations, multi-user collaborative investigation capabilities in reconstructed fire scenes, and advanced gesture recognition and interaction techniques to support intuitive and coordinated multi-user engagement. 

AI-based facility anomaly detection and predictive maintenance solution

Regional Innovation System & Education Program (2025 ~ 2026)

  • This project aims to develop an AI-based predictive maintenance solution that can detect early signs of equipment failure in industrial facilities, thereby reducing downtime and maintenance costs.

Finished Projects

Research on ICT Technology Discovery Linked to Digital Twin

Korea Electronics Technology Institute (2025 ~ 2025)

  • In this project, our lab is responsible for defining key data types and developing data visualization techniques for Energy Storage Systems (ESS), researching and developing 2D image and 3D object tracking technologies, and proposing XR-based utilization strategies for ESS, which will be summarized in the final report. 

Development of a Traffic Accident Video Analysis System

Korea Insurance Development Institute (2025 ~ 2025) 

  • We are developing core technologies for analyzing traffic accident footage, including video and image enhancement techniques, object motion and depth feature extraction, and the estimation of vehicle speed and steering angle based on object movement analysis. These technologies enable accurate reconstruction of vehicle behavior, such as speed and steering direction, at the time of accidents. 

Development of effective interaction technology and contents for immersive XR and 3D interaction for the social weak and manufacturing environments

Kumoh National Institute of Technology Academic Research Project (2024 ~ 2026)

  • Development of comprehensive XR/metaverse customized interaction technology and immersive content to support daily living and social needs of vulnerable populations, as well as effective interaction in the defense manufacturing environment. 

Development of an AI-based Competency Evaluation Model and Algorithm for QA Utilization in Customer Service Agents 

Kwangwoon University  (2025 ~ 2025)

  • This project aims to develop an AI-driven model inspired by the MBTI framework to evaluate and enhance the professional competencies of call center agents. By integrating NLP, voice analysis, and emotional intelligence detection, the system provides objective, data-driven insights for QA processes, enabling personalized coaching, real-time feedback, and proactive performance management. 

Development of spatial computing XR multimodal interaction technolog

S Corporation Research Contract (2024  ~ 2025)

  • Aiming to develop multimodal interaction technology based on spatial computing devices (Apple Vision Pro). 

Development of smart mirror and immersive content based tourism course and applied technologies

Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism, KOCCA (2023 ~ 2023)

  • Development of an immersive local history and nature tourism cultural content platform for smart mirrors based on regional tourism courses, along with the demonstration of an interactive service. 

The expandable Park Platform technology development and demonstration based on Metaverse·AI convergence

Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism, KOCCA (2021 ~ 2023)

  • Development of a scalable support platform through the commercialization of metaverse-based AI and immersive content convergence R&D. 

Intelligent digital contents production technology development and platform establishment project for Artificial Intelligence industry and Technology commercialization

Ministry of Science and ICT, Innopolis Foundation (2022 ~ 2023)

  • Development of AI, blockchain, and NFT-based digital content production support technologies for an intelligent digital content creation platform. 

The Development of a Music Based Functional Content Platform to Improve Social and Communication Skills among Individuals with Communication Deficits

Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism, KOCCA (2020 ~ 2023)

  • Development of a content support platform that collects and analyzes music activity and communication function status data to enhance social communication abilities, ensuring that youth and adults with communication difficulties can engage in daily life and fulfill social needs. 

Asian community heritage culture platform construction project

Gwangju Metropolitan City, Gwangju Information & Culture Industry Promotion Agency (2020 ~ 2023)

  • Development of a transmission model integrating cultural heritage with AI and cultural technology (CT) to contribute to the preservation of Asian cultural heritage and create value through utilization models.

Large-Scale AI-based industry-academia-research cooperation ecosystem

Ministry of Science and ICT, NIA (2024 ~ 2026)

  • Establishment of a hyper-scale AI convergence collaboration system based on private sector demand and fostering specialized talent and startup support.
